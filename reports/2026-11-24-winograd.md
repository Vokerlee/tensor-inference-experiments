# Precision, convolution transforms and bounded source reuse

The shape-only mixed control improves aggregate pixel fidelity while issuing
17.66% fewer input vectors than its matched eight-bit F(2,3)
control on four reused inputs. BF16 F(4,3) serves narrow layers; INT8 or FP8
F(2,3) serves the remaining layers. This is a useful stronger baseline for
online adaptation, not a demonstrated new adaptive policy or energy saving.
Ordinary causal rounding gives smaller gains. Simpler one-axis and support-only
rules do not produce a consistent free improvement.

## Workload and numerical contracts

The pinned SANA-Sprint0.6B DC-AE decoder maps32x32x32 BF16 latents to1024x1024
RGB. Its25 eligible dense3x3 convolutions account for3.6761T of4.1843T original
decoder MACs. The separate complete-request profile assigns70.649% of MACs to
this decoder for one prompt encoding and two denoiser calls. These are MAC
shares, not energy shares or a hardware compute-bound measurement.

Discovery reuses the first input of an earlier capture; follow-up reuses four
other earlier conditions. All five were already used in previous studies.
Discovery changes25 eligible layers; follow-up changes24 with at least128 output
channels, leaving the RGB projection unchanged. Do not pool these policy scopes.
[Complete condition identities](2026-11-24-winograd/input-conditions.json) retain
prompts, seeds and checkpoint revision. These small curated comparisons do not
establish population-level generation quality. No activation calibration or
network fine-tuning prepares any policy.

F2 and F4 denote F(2,3) and F(4,3). INT8 cases propose a K256/N128 integer mode,
with one current activation scale per row/K256 block and one prepared weight
scale per column/K256 block. Integer partial sums satisfy127^2*256<2^24;
decoded BF16 operands with FP32 output therefore implement their exact integer
partial oracle. Per-K scales are retired explicitly. INT4 codes do not imply
K512 throughput or packed storage.

FP8 controls use E4M3FN, current/prepared power-of-two block scales and the same
K256/N128 geometry. Decoded FP8 values are representable in BF16, but their
FP32 partial sums do not inherit the integer exactness proof. These controls
change weight representation too; their quality difference cannot be assigned
entirely to activation rounding. GPU implementations are numerical oracles,
not the target array's timing or bit-level accumulation specification.

## Rounding and rejected simplifications

Causal packed-power-of-two rounding uses one FP32 error state per row/channel,
two-byte direction/diagonal metadata and adjacent code decisions. It performs
no receiving GEMM. At INT4 F2 its four-input PSNR rises from23.8580 to24.0803dB;
this remains far from the40.1129dB INT8 nearest control. The rank1 INT8 gain is
also small. The floating completed-proxy variant uses more arithmetic and
retained codes or replay; its terminal fallback cannot be attributed to an
immediate-emission controller.

Local F4/group32/BF16-weight probes reduce aggregate receiving SSE to0.6862
(rank1) and0.5479(rank4) of nearest at INT4, with all25 layers improved. These
are different grids and weights from the full W8/K256 comparison, so they do
not predict its end-to-end outcome. Complete local outputs and all unfavorable
full-model cases remain available.

The one-axis shape-fit control worsens follow-up PSNR to23.7685dB at INT4 and
39.7342dB at INT8, despite favorable discovery. Applying one-axis lowering to
all layers improves these point values to24.5789/40.7558dB while increasing
issued vectors to81,018,880 per image. It is a quality/work tradeoff, not a
free energy optimization. Known one-axis identities and shape-aware selection
are not claimed as new.

Five nearest-upsample convolutions have exactly7/16 zero F2 planes in the
recorded K256 groups. Across the24-layer scope this represents5,275,648 of
62,423,040 vectors, or8.4514%, as potential whole-zero-input opportunities.
Their elimination still needs a realizable array protocol and gating.
Projecting the bounded metric onto producer-declared9/16 or25/36 support gives
mixed, small quality differences; it does not establish a stronger policy.
The oracle checks actual inactive values and retains the original product
layout. Algebraic zeros are not assumed valid for every rounded transform graph.

## Hardware boundary and prior work

The [controller ledger](../docs/plane-controller-cost.md) counts extra additions,
logical exponent adjustments, coefficient delivery and state access. M64/K256
requires64KiB FP32 state;256 parallel state updates imply2048bytes of logical
read/write access capacity per vector. The encoded LHS port is256bytes, while
256 original BF16 values require512bytes before conversion. Neither bandwidth
nor controller ports are free.

The [encoded-source ledger](../docs/winograd-encoded-retention.md) includes both
arguments, scales and state. F4/C1024/M64 needs2.25MiB for INT8 source codes
alone, exceeding the shared2MiB argument memory. M32 with four RHS planes and
double buffering fits the declared capacity model; result memory is a separate
2MiB. Full F4 W8 payload can reach36MiB, while BF16 F4 can require N tiling.
The general64MiB memory is on chip. Capacity fits do not establish DMA overlap,
ports, fill/drain overhead, output cadence or whole-layer activation residence.

BF16 versus INT8/FP8 arithmetic energy, zero-lane gating, weight movement and
partial-sum retention can reverse a packet-count ranking. An energy result
requires those costs. No GPU duration or allocated-memory measurement is used
as their substitute.

[Product-aware rounding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31641v1) already coordinates
errors and discusses reusable activation copies. [Tap-wise Winograd hardware](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.12982v1)
already treats transform/rate/buffer co-design; [live channel-balanced Winograd](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Chikin_Channel_Balancing_for_Accurate_Quantization_of_Winograd_Convolutions_CVPR_2022_paper.html)
already observes dynamic activation scales. [Winograd transposed-convolution hardware](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/9/2/286)
already exploits structural sparsity. The specific bounded approximation,
producer support and workload tradeoffs need their own comparison; none of
these measured points proves a new rounding principle or worldwide priority.
